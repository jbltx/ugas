[[overview]]
== Overview

This genre pack targets *team and athlete sports games* in the FIFA / NBA 2K / Madden mould — and
the arcade end of the family (NBA Jam, Rocket League-style momentum swings) just as readily: an
athlete defined by *physical* attributes and *on-ball skill* ratings, a *stamina reserve* that
drains with effort, and a *team momentum* meter that swings a match. Its signature is a
*stamina-driven performance coupling*: as an athlete tires, speed and finishing degrade
*continuously and proportionally* — and when the team is on top, a momentum surge multiplies their
offensive skill.

Within the link:taxonomy.html[genre taxonomy] (`family-sports`) it sits across the *traditional
sports simulation* and *arcade sports* subgenres — performance-driven play built on individual
athlete attributes and a team-wide meter — rather than *sports management* (which is roster/economy
strategy) or *sports-based fighting* (which the Combat pack covers).

Unlike the Platformer, Racing, and ARPG packs, the core specification ships no dedicated sports case
study, so this pack is designed from first principles. It still leans entirely on core constructs —
the *AttributeBased* magnitude type (link:../../SPEC.adoc[core spec] §9.4.2) for the fatigue
coupling, the modifier `Channel` mechanism (§5.3) for the momentum buff, periodic Effects (§9.3) for
stamina drain and recovery, effect-granted tags for the athlete state machine, and an optional custom
`Execution` (§9.5) for shot resolution. It ships the matching schema-conformant Attributes, Tags,
Abilities, and Effects in `entities/`, plus a worked athlete in `entities/gameplay_controller.yaml`.

[[relationship-to-core-spec]]
== Relationship to the core specification

This document is *additive*. It defines genre-specific Attributes, Tags, Abilities, and
Effects on top of the core Universal Gameplay Ability System specification.

* It MUST NOT redefine, override, or contradict any concept in the core spec.
* All entities in `entities/` validate against the same `schemas/*.json` as the core.
* It *reuses* the core lifecycle tags (`State.Alive`, `State.Combat`, `State.Cutscene`) by reference,
  and adds sports-specific states (`Athlete.State.Fatigued`, `Team.State.HighMomentum`, …).
* Athlete and team state is mutated *only through Effects* (core spec §3.1) — abilities never write
  tags directly; they gate on tags and apply Effects.
* The fatigue coupling is the core `AttributeBased` magnitude type (§9.4.2) and the momentum buff is
  the core modifier `Channel` mechanism (§5.3) — neither is a new construct.

[[genre-attributes]]
== Genre Attributes

Defined in `entities/attribute_set.yaml` as the `AthletePerformanceSet` set. The base values describe
a competent all-round outfield athlete, so the set is playable as shipped.

[cols="1,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Attribute | Category | Role

| `MaxStamina` / `Stamina` | Statistic / Resource | `Stamina` is clamped to `[0, MaxStamina]`; drained by sprinting and ability costs, recovered out of effort. The *backing attribute* of the fatigue coupling.
| `Speed` | Statistic | Top movement speed; scaled *down* by `GE_Fatigue` in the `Fatigue` channel as `Stamina` falls.
| `Acceleration` | Statistic | How quickly the athlete reaches top `Speed`.
| `Strength` | Statistic | Physical power for shielding, tackling, and aerial duels.
| `PassAccuracy` | Statistic | Likelihood a pass finds its target; scaled *up* by `GE_MomentumBoost` in the `Momentum` channel.
| `ShotPower` | Statistic | Raw power behind a shot; boosted by `GE_MomentumBoost` (`Momentum` channel) and read by `ExecCalc_ShotResolution`.
| `ShotAccuracy` | Statistic | Shot placement precision; scaled *down* by `GE_Fatigue` in the `Fatigue` channel as `Stamina` falls.
| `Tackling` | Statistic | Ability to win the ball cleanly in a challenge.
| `Composure` | Statistic | Steadiness under pressure; resists the defender-pressure penalty in `ExecCalc_ShotResolution`.
| `MaxMomentum` / `Momentum` | Statistic / Resource | `Momentum` is clamped to `[0, MaxMomentum]`; the team swing meter, spent into `GE_MomentumBoost` when it peaks.
| `EffectiveShotPower`, `GoalProbability` | Meta | Runtime telemetry written by `ExecCalc_ShotResolution`; the resolved shot power and scoring chance for UI/analytics.
|===

[[genre-fatigue-coupling]]
== Stamina-driven performance coupling (the core sports mechanic)

The pulse of a sports match is *stamina*: a fresh athlete is fast and clinical, a spent one is
laboured and wayward. This pack expresses that not as a step-function debuff but as a *continuous
coupling* — the showcase for the core `AttributeBased` magnitude type.

=== The fatigue coupling via AttributeBased magnitudes

`GE_Fatigue` (`entities/effect_fatigue.yaml`) carries two `Multiply` modifiers whose magnitudes are
not constants but are *derived from the live `Stamina` attribute*. The core formula (§9.4.2) is:

[stem]
++++
m = (\text{BackingAttribute} + \text{PreMultiplyAdditive}) \times \text{Coefficient} + \text{PostMultiplyAdditive}
++++

With `BackingAttribute: Stamina` and `PreMultiplyAdditive: -100` (so the term is
latexmath:[\text{Stamina} - \text{MaxStamina}]), the two modifiers are:

* `Speed`: latexmath:[m = (\text{Stamina} - 100) \times 0.005]
* `ShotAccuracy`: latexmath:[m = (\text{Stamina} - 100) \times 0.004]

Each modifier is a `Multiply` in the `Fatigue` channel, so its pipeline factor is
latexmath:[1 + m] (§5.3). At full stamina the factor is exactly latexmath:[1.0] — *no penalty when
fresh* — and it shrinks smoothly as the reserve drains. The deeper the fatigue, the larger the bite:

[cols="1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| `Stamina` | `Speed` factor latexmath:[(1+m)] | resulting `Speed` (base 100)

| 100 | latexmath:[1.00] | latexmath:[100.0]
| 60  | latexmath:[0.80] | latexmath:[80.0]
| 40  | latexmath:[0.70] | latexmath:[70.0]
| 0   | latexmath:[0.50] | latexmath:[50.0]
|===

.Worked example (`entities/gameplay_controller.yaml`)
A striker worn down to latexmath:[\text{Stamina} = 40]:

* `Speed`: latexmath:[100 \times \left(1 + (40 - 100)\times 0.005\right) = 100 \times 0.70 = 70.0]
* `ShotAccuracy`: latexmath:[75 \times \left(1 + (40 - 100)\times 0.004\right) = 75 \times 0.76 = 57.0]

These are exactly the `CurrentValue` entries recorded for the example athlete. Because the magnitude
re-reads `Stamina` on each evaluation, *no calculation code is involved* — the coupling is pure data.

=== The fatigue threshold and the Fatigued gate

The coupling above is smooth, but a hard *state* gate is what stops a tired athlete from sprinting on.
When `Stamina` crosses a low threshold the engine applies `GE_Fatigue`, which — besides the coupling
— grants `Athlete.State.Fatigued`; when `Stamina` recovers above the threshold the engine removes it.
That threshold check is engine logic, since an attribute comparison cannot be an activation tag — the
same pattern the shooter pack uses for "reload only when not full". `Athlete.State.Fatigued` sits in
the `ActivationBlockedTags` of `GA_Sprint` and `GA_SkillMove`, so an exhausted athlete can neither
sprint nor pull off flair until they have recovered.

=== Team momentum via a multiplicative channel

The attacking counterpart to fatigue is *team momentum*. `GE_MomentumBoost`
(`entities/effect_momentum_boost.yaml`) is applied when the `Momentum` meter peaks and puts a
`Multiply` of latexmath:[+0.20] on the offensive skill stats in a dedicated `Momentum` channel,
granting `Team.State.HighMomentum`. Because `Momentum` is a *different* channel from `Fatigue`, the
two compose multiplicatively (§5.3): a stat can be fatigue-coupled and momentum-boosted at the same
time without either cancelling the other.

* `ShotPower`: latexmath:[80 \times (1 + 0.20) = 96.0]
* `PassAccuracy`: latexmath:[70 \times (1 + 0.20) = 84.0]

In the worked athlete the affected stats each sit in exactly one channel, so these factors apply
cleanly — but a `ShotPower` that were *also* fatigue-coupled would read
latexmath:[80 \times \text{Fatigue} \times \text{Momentum}], the channels multiplying as the pipeline
prescribes.

[[genre-shot-resolution]]
== Shot resolution

`GE_ShotAttempt` (`entities/effect_shot_attempt.yaml`) is the instant payload `GA_Shoot` applies at
goal. A shot outcome is not a single subtraction: it couples the shooter's (already fatigue-coupled)
`ShotPower` and `ShotAccuracy` with the live `Stamina` reserve and the *defender pressure* on the
shooter, then resolves whether the ball beats the keeper. That multi-attribute, source-vs-target
logic can't be a static modifier, so the effect delegates to a custom `Execution`
(`ExecCalc_ShotResolution`, §9.5) that writes `EffectiveShotPower` and `GoalProbability` (both `Meta`)
for telemetry and UI. This is the sports analogue of the Racing pack's traction calculator and the
Shooter pack's hit resolution — the optional seam where a genre's signature math plugs into UGAS. A
pure-data adopter can skip it; the fatigue coupling and momentum buff need no engine code at all.

[[genre-tags]]
== Genre Tags

Defined in `entities/tag_registry.yaml` (additive only):

* *Athlete states* — `Athlete.State.Sprinting|Fatigued|Injured`.
* *Team states* — `Team.State.HighMomentum`.
* *Team affiliation* — `Team.Friendly|Hostile`.
* *Player roles* — `Role.Striker|Midfielder|Defender|Goalkeeper`.
* *Ability types* — `Ability.Type.Sprint|Pass|Shoot|Tackle|Skill`.

[[genre-abilities]]
== Genre Abilities

* `entities/ability_sprint.yaml` — `GA_Sprint`: hold to run flat-out; applies the periodic
  `GE_SprintDrain` and ends on input release (`WaitInputRelease`). Blocked while
  `Athlete.State.Fatigued`.
* `entities/ability_pass.yaml` — `GA_Pass`: play the ball to a friendly receiver. Costs `Stamina`
  (`Cost: GE_PassCost`) on a short cooldown (`GE_PassCooldown`); `PassAccuracy` governs the outcome.
* `entities/ability_shoot.yaml` — `GA_Shoot`: strike at goal. Costs `Stamina` (`Cost: GE_ShootCost`)
  on a cooldown (`GE_ShootCooldown`); traces to goal and applies `GE_ShotAttempt`.
* `entities/ability_tackle.yaml` — `GA_Tackle`: challenge a `Team.Hostile` ball-carrier. Costs
  `Stamina` (`Cost: GE_TackleCost`) on a cooldown (`GE_TackleCooldown`); `Tackling`/`Strength` decide
  the duel.
* `entities/ability_skill_move.yaml` — `GA_SkillMove`: a technical dribble. Costs `Stamina`
  (`Cost: GE_SkillMoveCost`) on a cooldown (`GE_SkillMoveCooldown`); blocked while
  `Athlete.State.Fatigued`.

The trivial cost/cooldown effects (`GE_PassCost`, `GE_ShootCost`, `GE_TackleCost`,
`GE_SkillMoveCost`, and their `*Cooldown` siblings) are a flat `Stamina` cost and a cooldown tag; the
pack references them by name rather than shipping a file for each, the same convention the Racing and
Shooter packs use.

[[genre-effects]]
== Genre Effects

* `entities/effect_fatigue.yaml` — `GE_Fatigue`: infinite; the `AttributeBased` `Speed`/`ShotAccuracy`
  reduction (the signature coupling); grants `Athlete.State.Fatigued`.
* `entities/effect_sprint_drain.yaml` — `GE_SprintDrain`: `HasDuration` + `Period`; subtracts
  `Stamina` every tick while sprinting; grants `Athlete.State.Sprinting`.
* `entities/effect_stamina_recover.yaml` — `GE_StaminaRecover`: infinite + `Period`; restores
  `Stamina` while not sprinting.
* `entities/effect_momentum_boost.yaml` — `GE_MomentumBoost`: `HasDuration`; `+20%` to
  `ShotPower`/`PassAccuracy` in the `Momentum` channel; grants `Team.State.HighMomentum`.
* `entities/effect_injury.yaml` — `GE_Injury`: `HasDuration` debuff; flat `Speed`/`Acceleration`
  penalties and a lower `MaxStamina` ceiling; grants `Athlete.State.Injured`.
* `entities/effect_shot_attempt.yaml` — `GE_ShotAttempt`: instant; runs the `ExecCalc_ShotResolution`
  `Execution` to resolve the shot and write `EffectiveShotPower`/`GoalProbability`.

[[using-this-pack]]
== Using this pack

. Copy `genres/sports/` into your project (or load `entities/` directly via the
  `ugas-schema-author` skill).
. Tune the athlete base values in `AthletePerformanceSet` first — and tune the fatigue coupling by
  adjusting the `Coefficient` on each `GE_Fatigue` modifier (a larger coefficient means a steeper
  drop-off as `Stamina` falls). Add player archetypes as `Role.*` tags and per-position stat tweaks.
. Author new conditioning or buff modifiers by choosing the right `Channel`: stamina-derived
  penalties belong in `Fatigue`, team swings in `Momentum`; reserve a custom `Execution` for
  multi-attribute resolution like shot outcomes.
. Implement the one optional `ExecCalc_ShotResolution` hook in your engine if you want
  defender-pressure-aware shot resolution; everything else — fatigue coupling, momentum buff, stamina
  economy — is pure data.
. Add new states as `Athlete.State.*` / `Team.State.*` tags granted by Effects (never mutate tags
  directly), and new techniques as Effects + Abilities.
. Validate with `python scripts/validate_schema_examples.py` before committing.
