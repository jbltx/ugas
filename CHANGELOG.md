## 1.0.0-draft.4

### Minor Changes

- 0594a6c: [Added] Casual genre pack (`genres/casual/`): an additive casual specification centered on the idle/incremental loop plus a schema-conformant template — `IdleEconomySet` (unbounded soft `Currency`, the aggregated `CurrencyPerSecond` income rate, `PrestigeMultiplier`, an `Energy`/`MaxEnergy` gate, `Stage`/`Level` progression, and idle telemetry), a casual tag taxonomy (`Meta.State.Prestiged`/`PrestigeReady`, `Booster.State.DoubleIncome`, `Generator.Type.*`, `Ability.Type.Tap|Buy|Prestige|Play`, `Session.State.OutOfEnergy`), and the signature mechanic — exponential economic growth from multiplicative `Generators` (flat) × `Prestige` × `Booster` channels on `CurrencyPerSecond` feeding a periodic `GE_PassiveIncome` tick (Infinite + Period, AttributeBased `+Currency` from `CurrencyPerSecond`) — with the key effects (`GE_Generator_Mine`/`GE_Generator_Factory`, `GE_PrestigeBoost`, `GE_DoubleIncome`, `GE_EnergyRegen`, `GE_TapReward`) and abilities (`GA_Tap`, `GA_BuyGenerator`, `GA_Prestige`, `GA_Play` with its shooter-style energy cost-gate), an `ExecCalc_OfflineProgress` execution seam for offline "welcome back" payouts, a touch-first input layer, and a worked idle-save controller (3 mines + 1 factory + prestige + a double-income booster → `CurrencyPerSecond` = 65 × 3.0 × 2.0 = 390). Designed from first principles as a ready-to-extend pack (#36).
- 6e81c83: [Added] Combat genre pack (`genres/combat/`): an additive fighting/brawler/hack-and-slash specification plus a schema-conformant template — `FighterCombatSet` (health, a poise/stagger meter, a guard/block-stamina meter, a super gauge, and frame-data feel stats), a combat tag taxonomy (frame-data + stagger states, startup/active/recovery move phases, ability types, hitzones, teams), the signature melee exchange (frame-data hitstun/blockstun states that block actions, a poise → stagger threshold that applies `GE_Stagger`, a `Block` damage channel that reduces incoming damage and slows movement, and combo cancels via `CancelAbilitiesWithTags` / `BlockAbilitiesWithTags`), light/heavy/block/special abilities with a meter-cost super and super-freeze lockout, a custom `ExecCalc_MeleeResolution` Execution coupling AttackPower, Defense, the Block channel, poise/guard damage, and super gain into `EffectiveDamage`, a gamepad input layer, and a worked mid-block fighter controller. Designed from first principles as a ready-to-extend pack, deliberately distinct from the Shooter pack (#38).
- 5ebf8b1: [Added] Puzzle genre pack (`genres/puzzle/`): an additive tile-matching specification plus a schema-conformant template — `PuzzleBoardSet` (a `Score` resource, a `MovesRemaining` / `TimeRemaining` economy, `ComboMultiplier`, `ChainLength`, and match telemetry), a puzzle tag taxonomy (board states, level phases, tile types, powerups, ability types), the signature match-scoring pipeline that multiplies a base match through a `Combo` channel (consecutive matches, via a window-refreshing `HasDuration` `GE_ComboBuild`) and a `Chain` channel (cascade depth, via `GE_CascadeChain`) plus a move economy where the swap's move cost is what ends the level at zero moves, the key abilities/effects (`GA_SwapTiles` / `GA_ActivatePowerup` / `GA_UseHint`; `GE_MatchClear`, `GE_PowerupBomb`, `GE_TimeBonus`, `GE_ResetChain`), an `ExecCalc_MatchScore` engine seam that banks the per-match product (tiles × base × combo × chain), a Touch + PC input layer, and a worked mid-cascade board controller (Score = 100 + 200 + 600 = 900 at combos ×1/×2/×3). Seeds the Puzzle case study (§16.4) as a ready-to-extend pack (#41).
- 7099379: [Added] Sports genre pack (`genres/sports/`): an additive team/athlete sports specification plus a schema-conformant template — `AthletePerformanceSet` (physical stats, on-ball skill ratings, a stamina resource, and a team momentum meter), a sports tag taxonomy (athlete/team states, affiliation, roles, ability types), and a signature _stamina-driven performance coupling_: `GE_Fatigue` derives its `Speed`/`ShotAccuracy` penalties from the live `Stamina` attribute via the core `AttributeBased` magnitude type (Fatigue channel) so performance degrades continuously as the athlete tires, while `GE_MomentumBoost` multiplies offensive skill (`ShotPower`/`PassAccuracy`) in a separate Momentum channel — the two channels composing per the core pipeline. Ships sprint/pass/shoot/tackle/skill-move abilities (stamina-costed, Fatigued-gated), periodic stamina drain/recovery effects, a timed injury debuff, an optional `ExecCalc_ShotResolution` Execution seam coupling power/accuracy/stamina/defender-pressure into `EffectiveShotPower` and a goal probability, a gamepad input layer, and a worked fatigued-striker-on-a-momentum-surge controller. Designed from first principles as a ready-to-extend pack (#40).
- 453be82: [Added] Strategy genre pack (`genres/strategy/`): an additive RTS / 4X / tower-defense spec plus schema-conformant entities built on a single `UnitStatsSet` (unit combat stats — `MaxHealth`/`Health`, `Armor`, `AttackDamage`, `AttackSpeed`, `AttackRange`, `MoveSpeed`, `SupplyCost`, `BuildProgress`, `EffectiveDamage` — and a commander-scoped economy of `Minerals`/`Gas`/`Energy`/`Supply`), and a tag taxonomy of `Unit.Type.*`, `Armor.Class.*`, `DamageType.*`, `Unit.State.*`, `Ability.Type.*`, and `Faction.*`. The signature mechanic is threefold: a resource economy driven by periodic infinite effects (`GE_MineralIncome` at `Period 1.0`, `GE_EnergyRegen`), a damage-type × armor-class rock-paper-scissors mitigation matrix resolved in the `ExecCalc_ArmorMitigation` Execution (`GE_UnitDamage`, `GE_AreaStrikeDamage`), and tech upgrades that stack declaratively through an `Upgrades` modifier channel (`GE_WeaponUpgrade` ×0.15, `GE_ArmorUpgrade` flat +2). Abilities cover `GA_AutoAttack` (range-gated, AttackSpeed-paced), `GA_ConstructBuilding` and `GA_TrainUnit` (resource cost + build time, with `GE_Constructing` blocking a site's abilities), and the caster `GA_AreaStrike` (Energy cost, AoE Magic damage + `GE_Slow`); a worked Siege Tank controller shows the `Upgrades`-channel `AttackDamage` aggregation (30 × 1.30 = 39) and the matrix worked example (39 × 1.25 − 2 = 46.75 vs Light). Adopters implement the one `ExecCalc_ArmorMitigation` seam; everything else is data. Ships the canonical RTS point-and-click input layer (actions, `Command` action set, PC keyboard+mouse mapping, camera modifiers). (#39)
- cfac69d: [Added] Survival / Crafting genre pack (`genres/survival-crafting/`): an additive survival specification plus a schema-conformant template — `SurvivorVitalsSet` (needs hunger/thirst/stamina, a body-temperature comfort window clamped like the racing pack's `TireTemperature`, survivability, and an encumbrance/crafting economy), a survival tag taxonomy (`Survival.State.Starving|Dehydrated|Exhausted|Cold|Hot|Overencumbered`, `Item.Type.*`, `Resource.Node.*`, `Ability.Type.*`, biome/environment), and the signature _needs/vitals decay loop_: periodic infinite need-decay effects (`GE_HungerDecay`, `GE_ThirstDecay`, `GE_StaminaRegen`) plus engine-detected threshold cascades that drain Health (`GE_Starvation`, `GE_Dehydration`, `GE_Hypothermia` with a two-channel `Cold`/`Encumbrance` `MoveSpeed` cut) and a `GE_Overencumbered` encumbrance penalty, a gather → craft → build economy (`GA_Gather`/`GA_Craft`/`GA_Build`/`GA_ConsumeFood`/`GA_Drink`/`GA_Sprint` with instant restore and harvest effects), an optional `ExecCalc_SurvivalTick` seam coupling all deficits into one Health delta, a PC input layer, and a worked night-time survivor controller (cold + starving + overencumbered) showing the `-3.0` Health/tick cascade and a `300 × 0.60 × 0.75 = 135.0` two-channel `MoveSpeed` reduction. Designed from first principles as a ready-to-extend pack (#35).
- c415922: [Added] State Persistence protocol (§14): defines how active effect state is serialized and restored — remaining-seconds duration encoding, infinite effect provenance tracking, periodic execution mid-cycle capture, and execution policy queue serialization.
