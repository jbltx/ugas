[Added] Strategy genre pack (`genres/strategy/`): an additive RTS / 4X / tower-defense spec plus schema-conformant entities built on a single `UnitStatsSet` (unit combat stats — `MaxHealth`/`Health`, `Armor`, `AttackDamage`, `AttackSpeed`, `AttackRange`, `MoveSpeed`, `SupplyCost`, `BuildProgress`, `EffectiveDamage` — and a commander-scoped economy of `Minerals`/`Gas`/`Energy`/`Supply`), and a tag taxonomy of `Unit.Type.*`, `Armor.Class.*`, `DamageType.*`, `Unit.State.*`, `Ability.Type.*`, and `Faction.*`. The signature mechanic is threefold: a resource economy driven by periodic infinite effects (`GE_MineralIncome` at `Period 1.0`, `GE_EnergyRegen`), a damage-type × armor-class rock-paper-scissors mitigation matrix resolved in the `ExecCalc_ArmorMitigation` Execution (`GE_UnitDamage`, `GE_AreaStrikeDamage`), and tech upgrades that stack declaratively through an `Upgrades` modifier channel (`GE_WeaponUpgrade` ×0.15, `GE_ArmorUpgrade` flat +2). Abilities cover `GA_AutoAttack` (range-gated, AttackSpeed-paced), `GA_ConstructBuilding` and `GA_TrainUnit` (resource cost + build time, with `GE_Constructing` blocking a site's abilities), and the caster `GA_AreaStrike` (Energy cost, AoE Magic damage + `GE_Slow`); a worked Siege Tank controller shows the `Upgrades`-channel `AttackDamage` aggregation (30 × 1.30 = 39) and the matrix worked example (39 × 1.25 − 2 = 46.75 vs Light). Adopters implement the one `ExecCalc_ArmorMitigation` seam; everything else is data. Ships the canonical RTS point-and-click input layer (actions, `Command` action set, PC keyboard+mouse mapping, camera modifiers). (#39)
